      Why these components? The concept buffer, concept-to-lemma associative memory and lemma buffer were selected following the dysfunction sites studied by Stille et al. (2020). Perception and word comprehension also play a role in the WWT framework, but the emphasis here is on word retrieval for production. These five configurations illustrate the current model; they do not reproduce the paper’s experimental conditions or results. Perception is shown as an undamaged reference.

      About this visualisation +
      A real neural model. The traces are generated in Nengo with 199,400 spiking LIF neurons per task. Each simulation covers 1.1 seconds of model time; playback can be slowed down. Repeated identical runs reuse the result already computed in this session.
      Similarity is not probability. The bars show dot products between the neural buffer state and the eight word templates. Related representations may also show elevated values. A separate small marker indicates the active associative-memory entry. All 613 entries participate in retrieval; only these eight are plotted. Concept and phonological similarities retain the original WWT network structure. Lemma vectors do not yet share word-class features.
      Deliberately simplified. Sound forms are supplied as whole-word pointers. The IPA display uses a fixed broad transcription for each retrieved word. Acoustic analysis, neural syllable sequencing and a Dynart connection are not yet included.
